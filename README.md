# energy-climate-nexus

Individual research mapping the intersection of electrification, wealth, and climate hazard exposure across African countries. Published at [varshabsuresh.github.io/energy-climate-nexus](https://varshabsuresh.github.io/energy-climate-nexus/).

## Structure

Each country is a self-contained subfolder with its own scripts, data documentation, and page:

- [`senegal/`](senegal/) — population-weighted wealth and climate hazard exposure by department; settlement-level flood-resilience analysis (DiD/event study on nighttime lights)
- [`zambia/`](zambia/) — ward-level electrification and nighttime lights; district-level climate hazard exposure and risk

See each subfolder's `README.md` (or its published page) for full methodology, data sources, and what is/isn't included.
