# Assessing Energy Access & Climate Vulnerability

Individual research mapping the intersection of electrification, wealth, and climate hazard exposure across African countries, using open-source geospatial data (satellite nighttime lights, climate hazard rasters, census and survey data).

## Countries

### [Senegal →](senegal/)

Population-weighted wealth and climate hazard exposure by department, plus a settlement-level analysis of whether flood recovery (measured via nighttime lights) differs by electrification status.

### [Zambia →](zambia/)

Ward-level electrification status and nighttime lights, plus district-level climate hazard exposure and risk classification.
